# CoPilot Interview: AI interview assistant for Windows and macOS

**CoPilot Interview** is a desktop AI interview assistant and AI mock-interview app. It gives you live, structured answers during interviews and lets you practice with an AI interviewer built from your résumé. The free plan includes **15 minutes a day, no credit card, and it never expires**.

## What it does

- **Live answers (Candidate mode):** listens to the interviewer through your meeting app (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet) and shows a structured answer with the key points first, usually in about 4 seconds. Live captions show each question as it's spoken.
- **Coding & system design:** working solutions in your chosen language with the approach and complexity explained, plus structured system design answers covering requirements, components, data flow and trade-offs.
- **Dual AI:** compare two models' answers side by side.
- **AI mock interviews:** questions are generated from your résumé and the job description. You choose the level, the number of questions and the mix (behavioral, technical, system design, coding). Each answer gets a score, with what worked and what to improve.
- **Interviewer Mode:** for hiring managers. It suggests role-specific next questions, each with what it tests and what to listen for.
- **Résumé Builder:** tailors your résumé to a job description, exports Markdown or LaTeX, and drafts a referral message.
- **9 AI models**, chosen per question. Fast open models are free; paid plans add GPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok.

## FAQ

**Is it a browser extension?** No. It's a native desktop app for Windows and macOS that runs alongside your meeting app.

**Is there a free plan?** Yes: 15 minutes a day with no credit card, and it doesn't expire.

**Can I use it for practice only?** Yes. Mock Interview mode is included on the free plan.

**Is using it allowed?** Always follow the rules your employer or assessment sets for AI assistance. Some allow it and some don't.
